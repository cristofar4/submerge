Our world

We live as guests of the reef.

Coral restoration

Our nursery grows coral fragments that we replant on the house reef every month.

Powered by the sun

Solar arrays on the surface pavilion supply the resort, and seawater cools every suite.

Reef-safe by default

Only reef-safe sunscreen and toiletries, and no single-use plastic anywhere on site.

Before you go

Questions, answered.

Can't find what you need? Send us a message and our reservations team will reply within a day.

Do I need to swim or dive to stay here?

No. You reach your suite by a dry glass lift, and every room is kept at surface pressure. Diving and snorkelling are optional.

How do I get to SUBMERGE?

We arrange a seaplane from the international airport to our arrival jetty. The flight takes about 40 minutes and is included in every stay.

Is it safe to sleep underwater?

The structure is monitored around the clock by our engineering team, and every suite has a direct line to the surface pavilion.

Can children stay?

Children aged 8 and over are welcome in the Cliffside Villa and the Manta Residence.

What is included in the rate?

Daily breakfast, seaplane transfers, a guided house-reef snorkel and access to the Tide Lounge.
